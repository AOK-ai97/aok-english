Rule · Qoida
Modal perfects — o'tmish haqida taxmin va afsus
ShaklMa'noMisol
should have + V3afsus / tanqidI should have learnt coding earlier.
shouldn't have + V3qilmaslik kerak ediYou shouldn't have quit your job.
must have + V3deyarli aniq (90%)She must have studied hard — she got the job.
might / could have + V3ehtimol (50%)He might have missed the email.
can't have + V3bo'lishi mumkin emasThey can't have finished already — it's only 9 am.

Practice · Mashqlar
A Choose the best modal perfect. — eng mos modalni tanlang
1.He didn't get the job. He (should have / must have) prepared better.

Reading · O'qish · Reading Part 2 format
Read the descriptions of jobs (1–8) and the sentences (A–J). Which job does each sentence describe? There are two extra sentences. — 8 kasb — 10 gap (2 ta ortiqcha)
1. Drone Pilot — Uses drones to check crops, deliver medicine to remote villages or film events. Requires a licence and good spatial skills.
2. Data Analyst — Studies large amounts of information to help companies make decisions. Strong maths is essential, but so is explaining results simply.
3. Wind Technician — Installs and repairs wind turbines, often 100 metres above the ground. Fear of heights is not an option!
4. Online Tutor — Teaches students from different countries through video calls. Can work from home and choose own hours.
5. Cyber Security Specialist — Protects companies from hackers. Must stay calm under pressure, because attacks can happen at any time of day or night.
6. Elderly Care Worker — Helps older people live independently. As populations age, demand for this job is growing faster than for almost any other.
7. Content Creator — Makes videos, podcasts or posts for brands. Income can be unpredictable, especially at the beginning.
8. Genetic Counsellor — Explains the results of DNA tests to patients and families. Needs both scientific knowledge and empathy.

Reading · O'qish
F Read and write T (True), F (False) or NG (Not Given). — T — to'g'ri, F — noto'g'ri, NG — matnda aytilmagan
The Drone Pilot

Five years ago, Sherzod was working as a taxi driver. 'I should have studied more at school,' he says, 'but I was lucky.' One day he watched a video about drones used in agriculture and decided to take an online course. Today he is a qualified drone pilot. He flies drones over cotton fields to check the plants and spray them with water. A farmer who used to need a week to check his fields can now do it in two hours. Sherzod believes that many jobs will become obsolete, 'but if you are ready to adapt, new careers will appear.' Next year he plans to open his own company.

1.Sherzod used to be a taxi driver.
2.He studied drones at university.
3.He uses drones in agriculture.
4.Checking fields now takes a week.
5.He already has his own company.
