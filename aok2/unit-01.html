Rule · Qoida
Present Perfect — hayotiy tajriba (qachonligi muhim emas)
+I / you / we / they have ('ve) + V3I've visited Bukhara.
he / she / it has ('s) + V3She's been to Turkey.
−haven't / hasn't + V3We haven't seen the sea.
?Have / Has + ega + V3?Have you ever flown? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

V3 = 3-shakl: to'g'ri fe'llar -ed (visited), noto'g'ri fe'llar — jadvaldan (go → gone, see → seen, fly → flown, eat → eaten).

ever / never · been / gone
ever — hech (savolda)Have you ever been abroad?
never — hech qachonI've never tried sushi.
been to — borib, qaytib kelganHe's been to Paris. (hozir uyda)
gone to — ketgan, hali u yerdaHe's gone to Paris. (hozir Parijda)
How many times…?I've been to Samarkand three times.

Reading · O'qish · Reading Part 3 format
Read the text. Choose the correct heading for each paragraph (1–5) from the list (A–G). There are two extra headings. — har bir abzatsga sarlavha tanlang (2 ta ortiqcha)
Your First Trip Abroad: Five Simple Tips

1. Have you ever travelled abroad alone? If not, your first trip can be exciting but also a little scary. Before you go, make a list of everything you need: your passport, tickets, money and a phone charger. Check the list twice. Many travellers have forgotten something important at home, and it has spoiled their holiday.

2. Don't put all your money in one place. Keep some cash in your bag, some in your pocket and a bank card in your luggage. If you lose your wallet, you will still have money for food and a taxi.

3. You don't need to speak the local language perfectly, but a few words will help a lot. "Hello", "thank you" and "how much?" are enough to start. Local people are usually happy when tourists try to speak their language.

4. Hotels in the city centre are often expensive. Hostels and guest houses are cheaper, and you can meet other young travellers there. Read reviews online before you book, and look at the photos carefully.

5. Finally, don't try to see everything in two days. Choose three or four places you really want to visit. Sit in a café, watch people, and enjoy the moment. Sometimes the best memories come from slow days, not busy ones.

F Read and write T (True) or F (False). — o'qing: to'g'ri — T, noto'g'ri — F
My First Trip Abroad

Last summer I travelled abroad for the first time. My family and I went to Georgia. It was a long journey: first we took a taxi to the airport, then we had a four-hour flight. Our flight had a two-hour delay, so we were very tired when we arrived. We stayed in a small hotel near the coast. Every day we went sightseeing with a tour guide. I have never seen such beautiful mountains! I bought souvenirs for all my friends. I have already been to Georgia, so next year I want to visit Turkey.

1.It was the writer's first trip abroad.
2.They travelled to Georgia by train.
3.Their flight was late.
4.They stayed in a hotel in the mountains.
5.The writer has been to Turkey before.
