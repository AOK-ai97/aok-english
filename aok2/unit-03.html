Rule · Qoida
First Conditional — real shart (kelajak)
If + Present Simple, will + V1If you revise, you'll pass the exam.
will + V1 if + Present SimpleYou'll pass the exam if you revise. (vergul yo'q)
−If you don't sleep, you won't remember anything.
?What will you do if you fail?
unless = if … notUnless you study, you'll fail. = If you don't study, you'll fail.

⚠️ If qismida will ishlatilmaydi: If it will rain rains, we'll stay at home.

Reading · O'qish · Reading Part 5 format
Read the text. Complete the summary with ONE word from the text (1–4). Then choose A, B, C or D (5–6). — xulosani matndagi BITTA so'z bilan to'ldiring; keyin A–D
Should School Start Later?

In many countries, school starts at eight o'clock or even earlier. But scientists say this may be a mistake. Research shows that teenagers' bodies work on a different clock from adults'. Most teenagers feel sleepy late at night and find it very difficult to wake up early. If they get up at six, they often come to school tired.

In 2016, a group of schools in the United States moved their first lesson from 7:30 to 8:45. The results were surprising. Students slept about forty minutes more every night. Their marks in maths and science went up, and fewer students were late. Teachers also said that classes were calmer.

However, not everyone agrees with the idea. Some parents start work early and want to take their children to school on the way. Bus companies say later timetables cost more money. And if school starts later, it will also finish later, which means that students will have less time for sport and other activities in the afternoon.

Experts say there is no perfect answer. But they agree on one thing: teenagers need between eight and ten hours of sleep. If they don't get enough sleep, they won't learn well, whatever time school starts.

Summary
Scientists believe teenagers have a different body (1) ______ from adults. When some American schools started later, students got more sleep and their (2) ______ improved. But some people are against the change: later timetables are more expensive for (3) ______ companies, and students will have less time for (4) ______ after school.
5.What happened in the American schools?
A) Students went to bed earlier.
B) Fewer students were late.
C) Teachers worked longer.
D) Lessons became shorter.
6.What do all experts agree about?
A) School should start at 8:45.
B) Parents should decide.
C) Teenagers need a lot of sleep.
D) Sport is more important than lessons.
