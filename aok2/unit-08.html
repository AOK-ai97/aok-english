Rule · Qoida
Present Perfect Continuous — o'tmishda boshlangan, hali davom etyapti
have / has + been + V-ingI've been learning English for two years.
−She hasn't been feeling well.
?How long have you been waiting?
for / since
for + davrfor ten minutes, for three years, for a long time
since + boshlanish nuqtasisince 2020, since Monday, since I was six

Holat fe'llari (know, like, have, be) bilan Present Perfect: I've known Aziz for ten years. (have been knowing)

Friends for Forty Years

Rustam and Akbar have been best friends since they were six. They grew up on the same street in Andijan and went to the same school. They have known each other for over forty years now. Of course, they have argued sometimes. Once they fell out over a football match and didn't speak for a month! But they always make up. For the last ten years they have been meeting every Friday to play chess. 'You can rely on a real friend,' says Akbar. 'He has always supported me.'
