Rule · Qoida
Second Conditional — xayoliy / real bo'lmagan shart
If + Past Simple, would ('d) + V1If I had a lot of money, I'd open a café.
−If he didn't work so hard, he wouldn't be tired.
?What would you do if you were a millionaire?
were — barcha shaxslar uchunIf I were you, I'd take the job. (maslahat)

1st — real ehtimol: If I get the job, I'll buy a car. 2nd — xayol / ehtimoli kam: If I got a job in Google, I'd buy a house in London.

My Dream Job

Sevara is sixteen and she dreams of becoming a pilot. 'If I were a pilot, I would see the whole world,' she says. Her parents want her to be a doctor because the salary is good. But Sevara isn't interested in medicine. She has already found a flying school in Tashkent. The course takes two years and it is expensive. 'If I got a scholarship, I would start next year,' she explains. Now she is studying English and physics every day, because pilots need both.
