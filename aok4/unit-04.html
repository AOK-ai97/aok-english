Rule · Qoida
Participle clauses — gapni qisqartirish (C1 uslub)
-ing — bir vaqtda / sababLiving in a megacity, she spends two hours commuting. (= Because she lives…)
Having + V3 — oldin bo'lgan ishHaving moved to Tashkent, he found a better job. (= After he had moved…)
V3 (passive)Built in 1977, the metro is still the city's pride. (= It was built…)
Not + participleNot knowing the area, we got lost.

⚠️ Ikkala qismning egasi bir xil bo'lishi shart: Walking home, the rain started. → Walking home, I got caught in the rain.

Grammar · Grammatika
C Rewrite with a participle clause. — participle clause
1.After he had finished university, he moved to Seoul. → university, he moved to Seoul.
2.Because she didn't have a car, she used the metro. → a car, she used the metro.
3.The tower, which was built in 1985, is now a museum. → in 1985, the tower is now a museum.
4.As I walked through the old town, I noticed new hotels. → through the old town, I noticed new hotels.
D Find the dangling participle and correct the sentence. — noto'g'ri participleni tuzating
1.Having finished the project, the city was proud. → Having finished the project, were proud of the city.
2.Built in 1966, the earthquake destroyed the old buildings. → The earthquake destroyed , built in 1966.
3.Walking along the river, the traffic was very noisy. → Walking along the river, heard very noisy traffic.

Reading · O'qish
F Read and write T (True), F (False) or NG (Not Given). — T — to'g'ri, F — noto'g'ri, NG — matnda aytilmagan
Copenhagen: Bikes Before Cars

In the 1970s, Copenhagen was a city dominated by cars, its streets congested and polluted. Having experienced an oil crisis, city planners decided to change direction. Over four decades, they built hundreds of kilometres of cycle lanes, many separated from traffic by kerbs. Today, more than sixty percent of residents commute by bike, even in winter. The benefits are measurable: air quality has improved, and health costs have fallen. Critics note that Copenhagen is flat and compact, making its model hard to copy in sprawling cities like Los Angeles or hilly ones like Lisbon. Nevertheless, inspired by its success, cities from Paris to Bogotá have introduced ambitious cycling programmes.

1.Copenhagen had heavy traffic in the 1970s.
2.An oil crisis encouraged change.
3.Most residents now commute by car.
4.Copenhagen's cycling model is easy to copy everywhere.
5.Paris has introduced a cycling programme.
