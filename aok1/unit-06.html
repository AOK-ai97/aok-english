Rule · Qoida
there is / there are — …bor
There is (there's) + bittaThere's a sofa in the living room.
There are + ko'pThere are two bedrooms.
−There isn't a garage. / There aren't any chairs.
?Is there a garden? — Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
Are there any books? — Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Joy predloglari
in – ichida
on – ustida
under – ostida
next to – yonida
behind – orqasida
in front of – oldida
between – orasida
opposite – qarshisida

The cat is on the bed. The ball is under the chair. The lamp is between the bed and the desk.

Grandma's House in the Village

My grandparents live in a village near Jizzakh. Their house is old, but it is very big. There are five rooms. There is a big kitchen and a living room with a long table. There are three bedrooms. There isn't a garage, but there is a beautiful garden. In the garden there are apple trees and peach trees. There is a cow next to the house, and there are ten chickens behind it. I love my grandma's house!

1.How many rooms are there?
2.Is there a garage?
3.What trees are there in the garden?
4.Where is the cow?
