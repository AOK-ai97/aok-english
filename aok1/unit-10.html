Rule · Qoida
Present Continuous — hozir, ayni paytda
+−?
I am (I'm) reading.I'm not reading.Am I reading?
he / she / it is reading.She isn't reading.Is she reading? — Yes, she is.
we / you / they are reading.They aren't reading.Are they reading? — No, they aren't.

So'zlar: now, right now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!

-ing yozilishi
ko'pchilik: +ingread → reading, cry → crying
oxiri -e: e tushadiwrite → writing, make → making
qisqa: undosh ikkilanadirun → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming

Har kuni = Present Simple (I walk to school every day). Hozir = Present Continuous (I'm walking to school now).
