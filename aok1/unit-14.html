Rule · Qoida
be going to — reja (allaqachon qaror qilingan)
+−?
I'm going to visit my grandma.He isn't going to travel.Are you going to swim? — Yes, I am.

Ko'rinib turgan narsa: Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.

will / won't — bashorat, shu zahoti qaror, va'da
BashoratI think it will be sunny tomorrow.
Shu zahoti qarorIt's cold. — I'll close the window.
Va'daI won't be late. I promise!
SavolWill you help me? — Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

So'zlar: tomorrow, next week, next summer, in 2030, soon

C Circle the best answer. — eng mosini tanlang

2.Oh no, I forgot my pen! — I (will / am going to) give you one.

✉️ From: Kamola · To: Feruza

Hi Feruza! Summer is coming and I'm so excited! Here are my plans. In July my family is going to travel to Khiva. We're going to stay there for five days. My dad booked a hotel in the old city last week. I'm going to take a lot of photos for my blog. The weather in Khiva is very hot in summer, so I'm going to take a hat and sunglasses. In August I'm going to stay at home and help my grandma in her garden. I think it will be a great summer! What are your plans? Are you going to visit me? I'll show you my photos! Love, Kamola

2.How long are they going to stay?
3.Why is she going to take a hat?
4.What is she going to do in August?
