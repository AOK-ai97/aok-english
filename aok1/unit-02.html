Rule · Qoida
Yaqin yoki uzoq? Bitta yoki ko'p?
bittako'p
yaqinda 👇this — This is a lamp.these — These are toys.
uzoqda 👉that — That is a door.those — Those are windows.
Savol va javob
What's this / that?It's a bed.
What are these / those?They're balls.

Qisqartma: that is = that's, it is = it's, they are = they're.
