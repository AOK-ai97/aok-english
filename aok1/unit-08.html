Rule · Qoida
Inkor va savol
−?
I / you / we / they don't playDo you play chess? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
he / she / it doesn't playDoes she like music? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

⚠️ doesn't / does dan keyin fe'lga -s qo'shilmaydi: She doesn't plays play. Does he likes like it?

Qanchalik tez-tez?
always 100% — har doim
usually 80% — odatda
often 60% — tez-tez
sometimes 40% — ba'zan
never 0% — hech qachon

Fe'ldan oldin: I often go swimming. Lekin be dan keyin: She is always happy.
