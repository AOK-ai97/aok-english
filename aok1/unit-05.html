Rule · Qoida
can — …a oladi (qobiliyat)
+I / you / he / she / it / we / they can swim.
−A fish can't (cannot) walk.
?Can it fly? — Yes, it can. / No, it can't.

⚠️ can dan keyin fe'l o'zgarmaydi: She can swims swim. He can to run run.

Fe'llar
🏊 swim – suzmoq
🕊️ fly – uchmoq
🏃 run – yugurmoq
🦘 jump – sakramoq
🧗 climb – tirmashmoq
🗣️ talk – gapirmoq

Our Clever Parrot

We've got a parrot. His name is Rocky. He is green, red and yellow. Rocky is very clever! He can talk. He can say "Hello!" and "Good morning!". He can fly, of course, but he can't swim. He can sing, too, but not very well. We've got a cat too. Her name is Mimi. Mimi can't talk, but she can climb and jump. Rocky and Mimi are friends!

1.Rocky is a cat.
2.Rocky can talk.
3.Rocky can swim.
4.Mimi can climb.
5.Mimi can talk.
6.Rocky and Mimi are friends.
