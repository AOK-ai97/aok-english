Rule · Qoida
Sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan
sanaladigan (countable)an apple → two apples, a carrot → carrots
sanalmaydigan (uncountable)milk, water, rice, bread, meat, tea — -s yo'q, a/an yo'q
sanalmaydiganni o'lchasha glass of milk, a cup of tea, a bottle of water, a kilo of rice
some / any · much / many
some — darak gap (+)There is some milk. There are some eggs.
any — inkor (−) va savol (?)There isn't any bread. Are there any bananas?
How many + sanaladiganHow many potatoes do we need?
How much + sanalmaydiganHow much rice do we need?
a lot of — ikkalasigaa lot of apples, a lot of water

Taklif: Would you like some tea? (savolda ham some!)
