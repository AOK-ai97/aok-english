Rule · Qoida
Past Simple — o'tgan zamon (tugagan ish)
to'g'ri fe'llar: +edplay → played, visit → visited, watch → watched
oxiri -e: +dlike → liked, dance → danced
undosh + y: y → iedstudy → studied, cry → cried
qisqa: undosh ikkilanadistop → stopped, plan → planned
noto'g'ri fe'llar — yodlang!go → went, see → saw, eat → ate, buy → bought (kitob oxirida jadval)
Inkor va savol — did
I / he / they didn't + fe'l (1-shakl)I didn't go to school. didn't went
Did + ega + fe'l (1-shakl)?Did you see the film? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

So'zlar: yesterday, last weekend, last summer, two days ago
