Rule · Qoida
was / were — edi (o'tgan zamon)
I / he / she / it wasI was at the park yesterday.
you / we / they wereThey were at school.
−: wasn't / weren'tIt wasn't cold. We weren't at home.
?Were you at the cinema? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.

So'zlar: yesterday, last night, last week, two days ago, in 2015

Yo'l ko'rsatish (buyruq gap)

Reading · O'qish

My Town Forty Years Ago — by Grandpa Hamid

Forty years ago, our town was very small. There were only two shops and one café. There wasn't a cinema, but there was a big park in the centre. On Sundays the park was full of families. There weren't many cars, so the streets were quiet. The children were always outside. There was a small hospital, but there wasn't a pharmacy. Now there are big shops, three hotels and a new station. The town is modern, but I think it was friendlier forty years ago.

1.How many shops were there?
2.Was there a cinema?
3.Where was the big park?
4.Were the streets noisy?
5.Was there a pharmacy?
6.What does Grandpa think?
