Rule · Qoida
to be — bo'lmoq (…man, …san, …dir)
+misol
I am (I'm)I'm Malika. I'm twelve.
you are (you're)You're my friend.
he / she / it is (he's, she's, it's)He's my brother. She's happy. It's a cat.
we / they are (we're, they're)We're a big family. They're my grandparents.
Inkor va savol
−?qisqa javob
I'm not sad.Am I late?Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.
She isn't (is not) angry.Is she your sister?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
They aren't (are not) here.Are they happy?Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
