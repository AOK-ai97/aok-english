Rule · Qoida
Kimniki?
I → myyou → yourhe → hisshe → her
it → itswe → ourthey → theirAli → Ali's

This is my bike. Her bag is red. Ali's phone is new. ('s = egalik: Alining)

have got — …i bor
I / you / we / they have got ('ve got)He / she / it has got ('s got)
I haven't got a camera.She hasn't got a bike.
Have you got a phone? — Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.Has he got a watch? — Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.
