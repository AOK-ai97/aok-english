Rule · Qoida
should — maslahat
+−?
You should drink a lot of water.You shouldn't eat fast food every day.Should I see a doctor? — Yes, you should.

Barcha shaxslarda bir xil: he should shoulds. Keyin fe'l 1-shaklda: should go.

must — qoida, majburiyat
must — shart!You must wear trainers in the gym.
mustn't — mumkin emas!You mustn't eat in the pool.
have to — kerak (sharoit)I have to get up early. She has to take medicine.

Never Give Up

Ulugbek is fourteen and he loves basketball. He plays for his school team and trains four times a week. Last year, during a big match, he fell and hurt his leg. For a month he stayed at home. The doctor said, "You mustn't play for three months. You should rest and do easy exercises every day." Ulugbek was very sad, but he listened to the doctor. Every morning he did his exercises, and he ate a lot of fruit and vegetables. After three months he went back to the team. This year his team won the city cup, and Ulugbek was the best player! He says: "You should never give up."

1.Ulugbek trains every day.
2.He hurt his arm.
3.The doctor told him to rest.
4.His mother helped him with exercises.
5.His team won the city cup.

My name is Rustam and I'm a tennis player. I train every day. I always eat vegetables and fruit and I drink a lot of water. I don't eat fast food and I never drink fizzy drinks.
