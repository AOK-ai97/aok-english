Rule · Qoida
Present Simple — har kuni, odatda
I / you / we / they playI get up at seven.
he / she / it plays (+s)She gets up at six.
-s, -sh, -ch, -o, -x: +eswatch → watches, go → goes, do → does
undosh + y: y → iesstudy → studies (lekin play → plays)
have → hasHe has breakfast at 7:30.
Vaqt
at + soatat 7 o'clock, at half past eight (8:30)
on + kunon Monday, on Sunday
in + kun qismiin the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening (lekin: at night)

Hafta kunlari: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday

In the afternoon, Ziyoda plays volleyball with her friends. In the (3) , she helps her mum in the kitchen. The evening is her favourite time, because all the family is at home.
