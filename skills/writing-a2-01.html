Writing · A2 · Task 1.1 (informal)Inviting a Friend

Do'stingizga taklif xati yozish: 4 qism, taxminan 50 so'z.
🔗Bog'liq ko'nikmalar
📖 Reading — Namuna xatni o'qib, qismlarini topasiz
🔤 Spelling — Xat iboralari: invite, weekend, can't wait…
🎤 Speaking — Xatdagi rejani sherigingizga og'zaki aytasiz
1Task · Topshiriq
Your friend Sam is coming to your city next weekend. Write an email to Sam. Invite him to do something together. Say where and when you will meet. Write about 50 words.
2Model · Namuna (5 qism)

GreetingHi Sam,

OpeningHow are you? I'm so happy you're coming to Tashkent next weekend!

InvitationWould you like to go to the Magic City park on Saturday? We can ride the boats and have ice cream.

DetailsLet's meet at the metro station at 11 o'clock.

ClosingCan't wait to see you! Write back soon.
Take care,
Aziz

3Build it · Har qismdan bittasini tanlang
4Write · O'zingiz yozing

Tanlangan qismlarni o'zgartiring: boshqa joy, vaqt va faoliyat yozing. Sayt avtomatik tekshiradi.

0 / ~50 so'z
  • ⬜ Salomlashish (Hi / Hello / Dear)
  • ⬜ Taklif (Would you like / How about / Why don't we / Let's)
  • ⬜ Joy (at / in / near …)
  • ⬜ Vaqt (o'clock / a.m. / p.m. / Saturday …)
  • ⬜ Xayrlashish (Take care / Best wishes / See you)
