Speaking · A2 · Part 1.1My Daily Routine

Kun tartibingiz haqida 30–40 soniya ravon gapirish: javob → sabab → misol → qo'shimcha.
🔗Bog'liq ko'nikmalar
🎧 Listening — Namuna javobni tinglab, tuzilishini sezasiz
🔤 Spelling — Kun tartibi so'zlari: get up, breakfast, usually…
✍️ Writing — Oxirida javobingizni 40 so'z qilib yozasiz
1Listen · Namunani tinglang
Tezlik:
What do you usually do in the morning?

Well, on school days I usually get up at half past six.

First, I wash my face and have breakfast with my family.

I always eat eggs and bread, because they give me energy.

Then I walk to school with my brother.

To be honest, I'm not a morning person, but I like my routine.

2Useful phrases · Foydali iboralar
Well, on school days I usually …Xo'sh, maktab kunlari odatda men …
First, … Then, …Avval … Keyin …
…, because ……, chunki …
For example, …Masalan, …
To be honest, …Rostini aytsam, …
I'm not a morning person.Men ertalab turishni yoqtirmayman.
3Shadowing · Takrorlab gapiring

Har gapni tinglang 🔊, keyin xuddi shu ohangda o'zingiz ayting va yozib oling. Namuna bilan solishtiring.

5Answer Makeover · A2 → B1
① A2
I get up at seven. I eat breakfast. I go to school.
② B1
On school days I usually get up at seven. I have breakfast with my family, because it's the only time we eat together. Then I walk to school, which takes about fifteen minutes.
